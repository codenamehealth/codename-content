## Layout
- Flat lavender background. One horizontal pool band, full width, y ~460-1530 (full res), red border top and bottom. Nothing above or below the band except text.
- 4 lanes, ~267px each, separated by red dotted floating lane ropes. Water is light blue with small white ripple dashes.
- Start pad: lavender "dock" on the far left (x 0-350) split into 4 cells by red lines, one runner standing in each cell.
- Runners are ~180-220px tall (big, readable). Lane 1 axolotl, lane 2 white duck, lane 3 teal penguin, lane 4 purple octopus. The duck is in the roster but is the interferer, not a racer: only 3 real racers.

## Runner motion
- Run/walk with small hop gait on platforms; jump gaps with a short arc. Octopus slides/flops. On ropes they walk upright slowly with arms out.
- Falls: knocked off, falls into the water, ends floating/swimming (the reference lets them float on the surface). Recovery: penguin swims back (drifts off-screen left 6.0-7.2s, re-enters from the left edge 8.6s along the lane rope and catches up by 9.0s). Octopus after the monkey-bar fall is pulled out by the penguin (stretchy-legs gag) 23.2-23.5s.
- Hill: climb slowly, then at the crest roll/tumble down the back like balls.
