# SPEC: Dd4TeRFSsqG (running-track obstacle race)

Source: igref/Dd4TeRFSsqG.mp4, 1080x1920, 34.13s, 30fps. Same creator and characters as DerujF_BHAA (axolotl, white duck, penguin, octopus), on a running track instead of a pool. Studied at 10fps over the whole reel. The camera was measured by profile correlation, which is only indicative here: the long empty track stretches have no vertical features.

## Layout
- One horizontal band mid-frame. Red running-track border top and bottom (thick red strips with a white edge line), grey lanes separated by white lines. 4 lanes. Lavender outside the band.
- Start: the 4 runners stand in start boxes on the left (white vertical start line); a row of hurdles stands just right of the start line in every lane.
- Roster order: axolotl lane 1, duck lane 2 (the interferer, as in DerujF), penguin lane 3, octopus lane 4.

## Camera
- Locked 0-2.8s.
- Scrolls right after the leader at about 240-300 px/s (3-6s) and up to ~450 px/s at times.
- Slows on the climbing wall (~145 px/s, 15.3-17.2s) and the cargo nets (~190 px/s, 22-23.6s), and speeds up after each.
- Fast through the cones (~450 px/s, 26-29s), then pauses briefly at ~29.5-30.3 (only the penguin and octopus are on screen).
- Fast catch-up at 30.5-31.2 as the finish lines come in, then locked from ~31.5 to the end.

## Obstacle sections (in order)
1. 0-2.8 Start boxes + countdown.
2. 2.8-7.5 Hurdles: red/white striped upright hurdles, several per lane, staggered.
3. 7.5-13.3 Plain track (no obstacles; the sabotage plays out here).
4. 13.3-18.9 Climbing wall: one big tan wall spanning all 4 lanes, with coloured climbing holds. Runners climb its face (top-down view, climbing toward the top of the screen) and drop off the far side.
5. 18.9-21.0 Plain track.
6. 21.0-24.9 Cargo nets: grey rope nets on orange posts across the lanes. Runners crawl under them flat on their bellies.
7. 25.0-26.0 Plain track.
8. 26.0-29.5 Cone slalom: orange traffic cones in a staggered grid in every lane.
9. 30.5 Finish: double white lines, a white finish area beyond.

## Runner motion
Waddling run; hurdles are hopped. Falls are face-first flops onto the track, and the runner lies flat for a while before getting up. Under the nets they belly-crawl. On the wall they cling and climb.

## Duck (interferer)
- 2.8-5.5 flies along lane 2 next to the penguin.
- 5.6-5.9 swoops down onto the penguin and knocks him flat (he is already down once from a hurdle at 3.7); he lies 5.9-7.3 and drifts off left.
- 8.0-8.4 flies up to the axolotl; 8.4-9.9 grabs him, they wrestle (axolotl screaming) and fall off left.
- Gone 10-25.
- 25.3 flies in again in lane 2; 26-28 flies over the cones; 28.1 collides with the axolotl at a cone (orange blur), and the axolotl is left lying still from 28.3 (off-screen 29.5).

## Gags / events in order (one at a time)
- 3.7 penguin trips over a hurdle and flops; 3.9 octopus trips too and lies flat 4.0-5.4, drifting off left; penguin gets up 4.1.
- 5.6 duck knocks the penguin down again (lies to 7.3, off-screen).
- 8.4-9.9 duck grabs the axolotl; both off-screen left.
- 8.0 octopus back in from the left; 9.2 penguin back in from the left; 11.7 axolotl back in lane 1.
- 14.3 penguin and octopus reach the wall and climb; the axolotl climbs ahead near the top.
- 15.2-16.0 the axolotl slips and slides down the wall face. At 16.5-17.0 his arms stretch to grab the penguin and octopus (stretchy-arms gag), and at 17.0-17.6 all three tumble together over the top and land on the far side.
- 21.7-24.6 the axolotl gets stuck in the lane-1 net, lying flat. 22.0-22.8 the penguin and octopus belly-crawl under their nets. 22.9-24.6 the two cut up across the lanes to the axolotl at the top and pull him out (24.7).
- 27.1-27.3 the octopus clips a cone and is flipped out of his lane, landing back by 28.4. 27.4-27.7 and 28.7-29.2 the penguin trips over cones (flop, gets up).
- 28.1 the duck crashes into the axolotl at a cone; the axolotl stays down, out of the race.
- 30.9-31.2 near the line, the leading penguin does a belly flop and lies down asleep just short of the finish line (31.2 to the end). The octopus walks past him and over the line at ~32.0.

## Lead changes (approx)
2.8-3.7 penguin/axolotl; 3.7 axolotl (both others down); 8.4 octopus (axolotl grabbed); 9.5-13 penguin/octopus level; 14.5-17 axolotl highest on the wall; 17.6 penguin; 22-24 penguin/octopus; 25-29 penguin; 31.2 penguin stops short; 32.0 octopus crosses = winner.

## Winner
The octopus wins because the leader lay down before the line. 32.5 a small crown pops in above the octopus; 32.7 his face turns sad and awkward. 32.8 WINNER pops in with confetti and holds to the end. The penguin sleeps on the track beside the line throughout.

## Text
- "Choose your runner!" white text with a dark outline over the start boxes, 0-2.75s.
- Countdown, red with a white outline, in lane 2 beside the start: 3 0.2-0.7, 2 0.9-1.35, 1 1.6-2.1, GO! 2.3-2.75 (pop-in scale).
- No text during the race.
- "WINNER" black on a yellow starburst over lane 2 by the finish, 32.8-34.13 (1.3s).

## Audio
Continuous music bed with SFX on the trips, the duck hits and the win. Not yet analysed by onset like DerujF; to be done when it's built.

## Length
34.13s: 2.8s countdown, ~29.2s race, ~2.1s from the winner crossing to the end (WINNER card on screen for 1.3s).

## Notes for the Code Name build (not started, on hold)
- Same role mapping as r01 is possible: the duck becomes the slime (hops only). The stretchy-arms wall gag and the pull-out-of-the-net rescue need new poses; they would become physical versions (the climber falls and lands, the trapped guide wriggles out), as in r01.
- The ending twist (the leader naps short of the line) would need a sleep/lie-down pose for that guide. The KO lying frame exists; a PixelLab "lies down and falls asleep" render would be cleaner.
- New PixelLab likely: belly crawl x4, wall climb x4 (the fork_b10 climb sets have a vine baked in), lie-down/sleep x1.
- The winner can't be Cleo. The reference ends on a 1.3s card; Bennett's rule is a 2-3s hold, so it would be extended to about 2.5s.
