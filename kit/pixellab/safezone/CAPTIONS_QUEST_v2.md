# Caption + pinned comment rules v2 (Bennett, Oct 30 2026 1:39pm). Supersedes METRICOOL_RULES.md sections B and C. Effective now.

Goal: maximum comments. Every Instagram and Facebook post, caption plus pinned comment, is built to get people to comment QUEST. The hook is that we are giving away a Nintendo Switch 2 on Feb 1.

Mechanic (keep exactly this): comment QUEST and we DM you the entry link. Entry is free, no purchase, US only, 18+. One entry puts a marble with your name in every monthly race from then on. Five heat winners each get a founder box, the heat winners race for the grand prize. First grand prize is a Nintendo Switch 2, Feb 1.

Caption (FB+IG): one or two lines about the video, then the giveaway line with the CTA. The CTA is the last line and it is always "Comment QUEST and we'll DM you the link" (vary the wording around it, never the keyword). Make the Switch 2 the first thing people read in the giveaway line.
  Bennett's examples of the shape:
  - "We're giving away a Nintendo Switch 2 on Feb 1. Comment QUEST and we'll DM you the link."
  - "One marble with your name on it, a Switch 2 on the line. Comment QUEST for the link."

Pinned comment (first comment): restate the giveaway in one or two lines and end with the same CTA. Written so a comment reader with no context gets it:
  - "Nintendo Switch 2 giveaway, Feb 1. Free to enter. Comment QUEST and the link lands in your DMs."

Art: mention once, briefly, that the characters are hand drawn by Syeda Humna (name her, nothing more). It never competes with the giveaway line. Liza gets the credit on posts that show her Realm environments.

Platforms:
- TikTok has no comment-to-DM, so the CTA is "DM us QUEST for the link" or "link in bio".
- YouTube gets no Side Quest CTA at all, caption or pinned comment.

Hard rules: no hashtags anywhere, no em dashes, no "link in bio" on TikTok posts that have a product tagged, don't say "free cards" or promise free physical product, don't mention jars or supplements in the caption. The keyword is QUEST, not REALM, in all captions and pinned comments going forward.
